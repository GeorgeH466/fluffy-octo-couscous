## Handling and retention

* This report and the associated evidence artefacts are confidential. Store them
  in the encrypted vault (`cdeck vault`) and share only with named recipients.
* Evidence hashes are recorded in the append-only chain-of-custody log
  (`/opt/cyberdeck-arsenal/data/chain_of_custody.jsonl`). Verify integrity at any
  time with `cdeck hash --verify`.
* Retain the evidence for the period agreed in the engagement contract, then
  destroy it securely (`shred -u`).
